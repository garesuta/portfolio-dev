# Garesuta brand brief

Source brief from Noppadol "Teen" Chinonn, recorded verbatim. Every design and content decision for the portfolio answers to this document.

**Garesuta is a curious, thoughtful software engineer's brand—built around understanding complex business problems and crafting useful solutions.** Its cosmic identity connects your background in physics, data analysis, and software engineering.

Your core positioning remains:

> A software engineer who combines analytical thinking, thoughtful design, and engineering craft to build the right solutions for business problems.

## Brand personality

| Personality | What it means | How you express it |
|---|---|---|
| **Curious** | You explore what is unclear and ask how things work. | Ask meaningful questions and investigate the problem behind a request. |
| **Analytical** | You examine evidence and connect details to the wider context. | Explain observations, assumptions, and the reasoning behind decisions. |
| **Thoughtful** | You consider business needs, user experience, and technical constraints together. | Show how you evaluate options and make trade-offs. |
| **Craft-conscious** | You care about how a solution feels, works, and evolves. | Demonstrate clear interfaces, considered interactions, and maintainable code. |
| **Practical** | You choose an approach appropriate to the problem. | Balance quality, scope, delivery time, and future change. |
| **Approachable** | You enjoy learning and solving problems with others. | Communicate clearly, welcome different perspectives, and acknowledge collaboration. |

Your overall presence should feel **quietly confident, precise, and human**. Explain your thinking through concrete examples, and let your work demonstrate your expertise.

| **Person behind the brand** | Noppadol "Teen" Chinonn |
| **Professional title** | Software Engineer |
| **Brand essence** | Business understanding, expressed through thoughtfully crafted software |
| **Creative concept** | Discovering structure within the unknown |
| **Professional signature** | **Business understanding. Engineering craft.** |
| **Expressive tagline** | **Clarity in the unknown.** |
| **Working philosophy** | **Observe. Understand. Explore. Build. Refine.** |

The **dark universe** represents the beginning of solving a problem: incomplete information, hidden relationships, and unanswered questions. Your career provides the connection:

- **Physics** gives you curiosity, observation, and reasoning.
- **Data analysis** brings evidence and business understanding.
- **Software engineering** turns that understanding into something people can use.
- **UX/UI thinking** helps make the solution understandable and comfortable to interact with.

Your story is therefore: **"From uncertainty to understanding. From understanding to useful software."**

## Visual identity: Starlight

Use the universe, the Milky Way, and the solar system to express exploration, relationships, and focus.

| Visual element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Atmosphere** | Spacious, immersive, calm, and precise |
| **Main background** | Deep Void — `#090B0E` |
| **Supporting surfaces** | Night Surface — `#11151A` |
| **Primary text and actions** | Starlight — `#F3F1EA` |
| **Highlights and stars** | Silver Dust — `#C9CED5` |
| **Supporting text** | Soft Grey — `#ABB3BD` |
| **Lines and boundaries** | Orbital Line — `#303842` |
| **English typography** | Satoshi |
| **Thai typography** | IBM Plex Sans Thai |
| **Wordmark** | **Garesuta**, using Satoshi Medium with balanced spacing |
| **Symbol direction** | A restrained orbital path with one illuminated point |

The interface uses the monochrome palette, while astronomical imagery and planetary surfaces can retain their natural colours.

## Motion identity

Motion follows the problem-solving approach: begin with the wider context, investigate relationships, and move toward a focused solution. The galaxy-to-solar-system journey expresses that progression. Hover highlights suggest discovery; clicking brings something into focus; zooming out restores the bigger picture.

## Voice

Clear, conversational, and grounded. A characteristic sentence:

*"I start by understanding what the business needs, examine the assumptions, and shape a solution that works for the people using it."*

In project stories, make that personality visible through **the questions you asked, the decisions you made, the trade-offs you considered, and what you learned.**
