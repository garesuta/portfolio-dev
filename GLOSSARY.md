# Garesuta Portfolio

The personal portfolio of Noppadol "Teen" Chinonn, presented under the Garesuta brand: a dark universe a visitor explores to discover his work.

## Language

**Warp intro**:
The opening sequence on first load: white pixel dots stretch into streaks rushing outward, flood the screen white, and burst open onto Main.
_Avoid_: Loader, splash screen, intro animation, wormhole, tunnel

**Playground**:
The explorable 3D universe (galaxy to solar system) that visitors play with for its own sake; it holds no projects or site content.
_Avoid_: Universe nav, galaxy menu, 3D homepage

**Main**:
The home page: the hero and the about story, set over the live Playground.
_Avoid_: Home, landing page, About

**Lab**:
An interactive showcase of the techniques and libraries this site is built with, each one playable and explained.
_Avoid_: Motion snippets, playground, demos, under the hood

**AI-era SDLC**:
Teen's way of building software when AI makes producing the work fast, so the craft moves into the thinking: frame the problem precisely, make a data-driven decision, make it with AI drafting under direction, then refine until it's right, with refine weighted as heavily as make. AI is the instrument; Teen's judgement is the craft.
_Avoid_: AI workflow, vibe coding, agentic pipeline, AI-powered development
